time limit per test
3 seconds

256 megabytes
input
standard input

standard output

You are given a weighted undirected connected graph consisting of n n vertices and m m edges. It is guaranteed that there are no self-loops or multiple edges in the given graph.

Let's define the weight of the path consisting of k k edges with indices e1,e2,…,ek e 1 , e 2 , … , e k as ∑i=1kwei−maxi=1kwei+mini=1kwei ∑ i = 1 k w e i − max i = 1 k w e i + min i = 1 k w e i , where wi w i — weight of the i i -th edge in the graph.

Your task is to find the minimum weight of the path from the 1 1 -st vertex to the i i -th vertex for each i i ( 2≤i≤n 2 ≤ i ≤ n ).

Input

The first line contains two integers n n and m m ( 2≤n≤2⋅105 2 ≤ n ≤ 2 ⋅ 10 5 ; 1≤m≤2⋅105 1 ≤ m ≤ 2 ⋅ 10 5 ) — the number of vertices and the number of edges in the graph.

Following m m lines contains three integers vi,ui,wi v i , u i , w i ( 1≤vi,ui≤n 1 ≤ v i , u i ≤ n ; 1≤wi≤109 1 ≤ w i ≤ 10 9 ; vi≠ui v i ≠ u i ) — endpoints of the i i -th edge and its weight respectively.

Output

Print n−1 n − 1 integers — the minimum weight of the path from 1 1 -st vertex to the i i -th vertex for each i i ( 2≤i≤n 2 ≤ i ≤ n ).
